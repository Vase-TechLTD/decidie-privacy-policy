DeciDie Privacy Policy

```

Last updated: October 2, 2026

DeciDie is a digital decision-making app that helps users make decisions by rolling a virtual die. Users can enter a decision, event, or question and roll the die to receive one of six possible outcomes:

  • Do it
  • Skip it
  • Delay it
  • Ask someone
  • Roll again
  • Blank

Information We Collect

DeciDie does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

DeciDie does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personal information entered by users into the app.

How the App Works

DeciDie is designed to provide a simple decision-making experience. Information entered by a user is used only for the functionality of the app and is not used to identify the user.

Data Sharing

DeciDie does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

DeciDie does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Third-Party Services

DeciDie does not intentionally use third-party services to collect personal information from users.

Data Security

Because DeciDie does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information, there is no personal account information stored by DeciDie.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's functionality or data practices change, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those changes. Any updated version will be published at this location.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DeciDie, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play Store listing.

```
